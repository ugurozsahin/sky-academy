- y2-conjunction — Joining Words: gap sentence, slice the joining word; d1 and/or/but (3 bubbles), d2 because/when/if/that (3), d3 any (4); decoys hand-keyed per sentence so only one word fits

Unit conversion (`1 m = 100 cm`, `1 kg = 1000 g`, `1 l = 1000 ml`) is **not** a KS1 topic and the three Year 2
measures topics no longer ask for it: it is Year 3 non-statutory at the earliest, statutory in Year 4, and it
needs three-digit numbers where Year 2 stops at 100. Year 2 compares and orders, chooses the sensible unit, and
adds or subtracts within one unit. Bringing the comparisons inside 100 took two moves: a larger-unit draw in
kg or l, and smaller objects in g and ml. (`y2-length` needed neither — its cm comparison was already 10–99;
the m draw is an addition, not a rescue.) Owner's KS1 review, #298 slice 1; the rails are in
`tests/unit/curriculum.test.ts`.

## Difficulty convention
d1 = introduce (small range, visual support) · d2 = NC expectation · d3 = stretch / mixed forms.
Reception phonics adds a second, cumulative dimension (#14): the letters a difficulty may use follow the Little Wandle / Letters and Sounds order — d1 phase 2 only, d2–3 every single-letter sound — for the answer **and** the decoys. The pools are derived from the Sound Hunt banks in `src/curriculum/util.ts`, so the phase order has one home. Missions have 5 stages (Apprentice, Warrior, Master, Grandmaster, Legend); each year maps stages → (difficulty, speed) in `YEARS[].diffs/speeds`.
KS2 naming rule (#1041): plain curriculum titles (no emoji, no "!"), functional icons, no toy icons, and no emoji-counting pictures (objects, ten-frame, dots) except a fraction-of-an-amount d1; `tests/unit/ks2-naming.test.ts` holds it.
KS2 distractor rule (#1058): for a pick-one card whose answer is a whole number of 20 or more, or any money/decimal answer, at most 30% of cards may have a last printed digit or a leading digit that no other option shares — misconception decoys first (`src/curriculum/mistakes.ts`), `nearby()` only to fill; an estimate card (a number-line mark, an estimated angle) asserts only the last-printed-digit half. `tests/unit/helpers/decoy-leak.ts`'s `leakShares` measures it per topic.
