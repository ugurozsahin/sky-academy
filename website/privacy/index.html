Privacy policy

Sharpwise collects nothing about your child. There are no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no cookies, in the game or on this website.

This page explains what the game keeps on your device, the few things that can leave it, and who to contact. It covers the game in a web browser, the Android app and this website.

What we collect
Nothing. We never see your child's name, progress or answers.
Accounts
None. There is nothing to sign up for.
Analytics and tracking
None. The game contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and every build of it is checked for some.
Adverts
None.
Cookies
None. Neither the game nor this website sets a cookie.
Where progress is kept
On the device the game is played on. On Android, the device's own backup may also copy it to your Google account, as explained below. It never comes to us.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember how a child is getting on, the game saves these in the browser's own storage on the device, or in the app's own storage on Android:

A name
The first name or nickname your child types in, shown on screen and on certificates.
Progress
Stars, scores, coins, stickers, streaks and certificates, plus recent wrong answers and daily play totals, so the grown-ups' area can show where to help.
Settings
Choices such as sound, read aloud and the grown-ups' settings.

Up to four children can each have their own ninja on one device, and each has a separate save. None of it is sent to us. In a web browser the game also keeps a copy of its own files, so it works offline; those are the game's files, not your child's.

What can leave the device, and only when you choose

Apart from Android's backup and spoken words, both explained below, nothing leaves the device unless a grown-up sends it. A grown-up can choose to send any of these, each from the grown-ups' area or after its quick grown-ups' question:

The save code
A copy of one ninja's save, name included, for moving it to another device.
A progress summary
A short summary or picture of how a child is doing, with their name, sent through your device's share menu or copied.
A certificate
A certificate picture with your child's name on it, sent through your device's share menu.
A challenge code
A short code that lets a friend play the same questions. It holds no name.
Results and word lists
Today's Daily Sprint result and the spelling words still to learn, which you can copy to paste elsewhere.

Each one goes only where you send it, such as a message or an email of your own. We never receive it.

Reading aloud

The game speaks using a voice that your browser or device provides. Some of those voices are online voices run by the company that makes the browser or device. When one of them is used, the words being spoken go to that company to be turned into speech, under its own privacy policy. The game prefers those voices because they sound clearer.

With read aloud on, the game speaks by itself: questions, and praise that can include your child's name. With read aloud off, it speaks only when someone taps something to hear it, such as the question card, a shop item, a Daily Dojo challenge or a sticker. Those lines never include your child's name.

Read aloud is switched on and off with the speaking-face button at the top of the Sky Map, separately for each ninja on the device.

Android backups

The Android app lets Android's own backup include its data. If backup is turned on for the phone or tablet, Android may copy the game's save, name included, to a private folder in the Google account the device uses. Android can then put the save back when the app is installed again, or when that account sets up a new device. That copy belongs to your Google account, and we never see it.

To stop it, turn backup off in the device's settings (on many devices under Settings, System, Backup; the place varies), or play in a web browser instead.

This website, and where the game is hosted

This website and the web game are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the IP address of each visit, for security. We do not receive it.

The website has no cookies and no analytics, and loads nothing from any other company. One small script of our own opens and closes the Curriculum menu. It sends nothing anywhere and stores nothing.

If you email us

If you write to support@sharpwise.co.uk, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and keep them only as long as we need to help.

Who is responsible

Sharpwise is made by Inobil Technology Ltd, the data controller for everything this page covers. You can contact us at support@sharpwise.co.uk.

Your rights, and how to complain

Apart from any email you send us, we hold nothing about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You can ask us to see or delete your emails at any time. Everything the game keeps is on your device, or on Android perhaps in your own Google backup too, and you can clear it yourself: the support page shows how.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled personal information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
