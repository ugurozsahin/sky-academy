Sharpwise at a glance

Who it is for
Children aged 4 to 11, and the parents and teachers helping them.
School years
Reception to Year 6. Reception topics follow the EYFS Early Learning Goals; Years 1 to 6 follow the National Curriculum for England.
Subjects
Maths and English: number, calculation, fractions, measures, phonics, spelling, grammar and reading.
Price
Free.
Adverts
None.
Tracking
None. There is no account to make, and progress is saved on the device.
Where it plays
In any modern web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. After the first visit it also works offline.

How a round works

  1. A question appears at the top of the screen. It can be read aloud, so children who cannot read yet can still play.
  2. Possible answers float up from the bottom in bubbles.
  3. Your child slices the right bubble with a finger or the mouse. Right answers earn stars, and each island has new topics to unlock.

Reception to Year 6

Each school year is an island with its own topics, in the order children meet them in class.

  1. Reception

    ages 4 to 5

    First steps: counting, sounds and letters.

  2. Year 1

    ages 5 to 6

    Number bonds, adding, phonics and spelling.

  3. Year 2

    ages 6 to 7

    Times tables, money, time and tricky words.

  4. Year 3

    ages 7 to 8

    Hundreds, the 3, 4 and 8 times tables, fractions and Roman numerals.

  5. Year 4

    ages 8 to 9

    Tables to 12 × 12, decimals, area and coordinates.

  6. Year 5

    ages 9 to 10

    Millions, primes, percentages and angles.

  7. Year 6

    ages 10 to 11

    Long division, ratio, algebra and SATs practice.
