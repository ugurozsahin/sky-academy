How to play

A short guide for young players. Read it with a grown-up.

Slice the right answer

A question comes up at the top.

Answer bubbles float up from the bottom.

Swipe across the right bubble to slice it.

You can tap it instead, or use a mouse.

Tap the speaker, Read the question aloud, to hear it.

Hearts

The hearts at the top are your lives.

A wrong slice loses one heart.

From Year 1, missing the right bubble costs one too.

A missed bubble falls off the bottom of the screen.

Lost a heart? Clear the stage to get one back.

No hearts left? The game ends, but you can try again.

Stars

A mission has five stages.

Each stage gives you one, two or three stars.

Get every answer right to win three stars.

Your best stars stay on each topic.

Coins

Every right answer earns a coin.

Stars earn more coins, and so does finishing a mission.

Spend coins in the Ninja Shop on new looks.

Tap your coins at the top to open Ninja Rewards.

The Ninja Shop button is on that screen.

Stickers

Stickers go in your sticker album, on Ninja Rewards.

Collect 30 coins for your first sticker.

Two more come at 70 and 120 coins.

Win the rest by playing in new ways.

Beat Hammer Man, or play three days in a row.

Spending coins never takes a sticker away.

The Sky Map

The Sky Map has an island for each school year.

Tap your school year to start.

Each island has its own topics to play.

The stars by each island show how far you have got.

Ready to play?

Pick your year and slice your first answer.
